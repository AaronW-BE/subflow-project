In short. SubFlow is a private notebook for the subscriptions you already pay for. It records what you tell it, does the arithmetic, and reminds you before a renewal. It has no connection to your bank, your card, or the services you track, and it cannot cancel anything on your behalf.

What the app does

You enter your subscriptions by hand. The app stores them on your device, normalises the amounts to a monthly figure, converts between currencies using a built-in rate table, and can post a local notification before a renewal date.

What it is not

  • It is not connected to your accounts. The app has no access to your bank, card, or any subscription provider. It cannot detect a charge, start a subscription, or cancel one. To cancel a service, you must do so with that service directly.
  • It is not financial advice. Totals, per-day figures and category breakdowns are arithmetic on numbers you supplied. Nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy, keep, or cancel anything.
  • Its figures are only as good as your input. Currency conversion uses an approximate built-in rate table, not a live market rate, so converted amounts are indicative rather than exact.
  • Reminders are best-effort. They are local notifications scheduled by Android. Battery optimisation, Do Not Disturb, a powered-off device, or revoked notification permission can delay or suppress them. Do not rely on SubFlow as your only safeguard against an unwanted renewal.

Free and Pro

The app is free to install and use. The free tier tracks up to five subscriptions, offers a one-day renewal reminder, and displays banner ads.

SubFlow Pro removes the five-subscription limit, adds three- and seven-day reminder options, unlocks the spending history and category breakdown, and turns off ads. Pro is offered as a monthly subscription, an annual subscription, and a one-time lifetime purchase.
